## 🚀 The Problem: The "Manual Entry" Bottleneck
Running a busy kiosk or local pickup point is hard enough. When packages arrive, your staff spends precious minutes manually typing in tracking numbers and recipient names. 
*   **Human Error:** Typos lead to lost packages and angry customers.
*   **Slow Intake:** Long queues during peak delivery hours.
*   **Disorganized Shelves:** "Where did I put that small yellow box?"

## ✨ The Solution: KioskPackagePro
The high-performance, AI-driven mobile application designed specifically for the modern kiosk owner. We turn any smartphone into a professional-grade logistics terminal.

### 🧠 Smart AI Intake (Powered by Google Gemini)
Forget manual typing. Point your camera at any shipping label, and our advanced AI instantly extracts the **Recipient Name** and **Tracking ID**. 
*   *Result:* Intake speed increased by up to 400%.

### 📍 Instant Location Linking
Scan the package, then scan the shelf. Our built-in QR and Barcode scanner (jsQR) links the item to its physical location in milliseconds. 
*   *Result:* Zero "lost" packages. Find any item in seconds.

### 📱 Mobile-First, Local-First
Built for the floor, not the desk. 
*   **Lightning Fast:** Uses IndexedDB for local storage—no waiting for slow cloud syncs.
*   **Reliable:** Works even when your Wi-Fi is spotty.
*   **Intuitive:** A thumb-optimized interface that requires zero training for your staff.
